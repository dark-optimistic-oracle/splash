# 0tru Media Kit

## Brand

**Name:** 0tru

**Category:** Privacy-first optimistic oracle

**Core line:** Truth on chain, without bribery receipts.

**Short positioning:** 0tru is an optimistic oracle for prediction markets that keeps dispute voting private. Anyone can enter a result. Anyone can dispute it. If a dispute escalates to voters, zero-knowledge privacy hides vote contents and payout trails so attackers cannot verify bribery.

## Narrative

An attacker can try to enter an incorrect "truth" into a market. In ordinary public voting systems, they can also learn who to bribe next time. 0tru breaks that loop. The council of voters can still follow the truth because no outside party can prove who voted how or who received what payout.

## Visual System

- **Motif:** two black horses running through smoke and dust.
- **Meaning:** the public market sees the winning result, but the witnesses remain dark.
- **Shape language:** chunky rounded-zero logo, rounded cards, simple protocol annotations.
- **Texture:** charcoal grain, grayscale horse photography, high-contrast pink accents.

## Product UI Direction

- Use dark operational screens with pink for key actions, dispute states, and resolved truth.
- Keep market details dense and readable: proposed outcome, bond, dispute window, vote status, settlement result.
- Avoid talking about the underlying chain/provider in primary marketing unless needed in docs.
